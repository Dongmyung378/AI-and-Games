## RAVE

Sylvain Gelly and David Silver. “Monte-Carlo Tree Search and Rapid Action Value Estimation in Computer Go.” *Artificial Intelligence* 175(11), 2011, 1856-1875. [Google Research](https://research.google/pubs/monte-carlo-tree-search-and-rapid-action-value-estimation-in-computer-go/) · [DOI: 10.1016/j.artint.2011.03.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2011.03.007)

This paper motivates sharing action statistics across a subtree and blending the fast RAVE estimate with the slower direct Monte Carlo estimate.

## Hex-specific MCTS

Shih-Chieh Huang, Broderick Arneson, Ryan B. Hayward, Martin Müller, and Jakub Pawlewicz. “MoHex 2.0: A Pattern-Based MCTS Hex Player.” *Computers and Games*, 2014, 60-71. [University of Alberta manuscript](https://papersdb.cs.ualberta.ca/~papersdb/uploaded_files/1728/paper_2013-CG-Mohex2.0.pdf) · [DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-09165-5_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-09165-5_6)

This work informed the use of Hex-specific rollout patterns and the broader decision to combine search with domain knowledge.
